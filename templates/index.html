System Capabilities

Four Core NLP Modules

1. Structured IRAC Summarization

Condenses lengthy court transcripts into standardized Facts, Issues, Arguments, Reasoning (Ratio), and Ruling briefs using graph-based TextRank and legal salience priors.

Launch Summarizer

2. Key-Element & Entity Extraction

Automatically isolates bench composition, decision outcome badges (Allowed/Dismissed), cited constitutional articles, statutory provisions, and referenced case laws.

Inspect Extractor

3. Semantic Precedent Engine

Overcomes the limitations of exact-keyword lookup using dense legal ontology embeddings and query expansion, finding cases that share similar judicial logic.

Explore Search

4. Side-by-Side Case Comparison

Select multiple related judgments to inspect facts, issues, and dispositions side by side, computing doctrinal concordance or distinguishing factors.
