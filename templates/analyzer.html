Analysis Protocol & Guidelines

To achieve optimal inference accuracy with the MobileNetV2 model:

  • Clear Lighting: Ensure the fruit or vegetable is well lit without harsh shadows or lens reflections.
  • Centered Subject: Position the produce in the center of the frame so surface features dominate the 224×224 tensor.
  • Natural Angles: Rotate the item to show any suspicious spots, bruising, or fungal growth if inspecting potential spoilage.
  • Supported Classes: Trained on common produce classes (apples, bananas, oranges, tomatoes, peppers, etc.).
Image classification estimates visual freshness only. It cannot detect bacterial toxins (e.g., Salmonella) or chemical residues.
