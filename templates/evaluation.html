Evaluation Methodology & Ground-Truth Reference Standards

ROUGE Metric Implementation

Summarization quality is quantified against gold-standard reference briefs curated by legal scholars:

  • ROUGE-1: Measures unigram co-occurrence, assessing the retention of foundational legal terminology (statutes, names, core facts).
  • ROUGE-2: Measures bigram co-occurrence, validating preservation of multi-word legal doctrines (e.g. "duty of care", "basic structure", "therapeutic efficacy").
  • ROUGE-L: Evaluates the Longest Common Subsequence, testing the preservation of sentence flow and syntactic structure without sentence twisting.

Precedent Search Evaluation

Information retrieval performance is validated against standardized benchmark queries representing diverse areas of jurisprudence:

  • Precision@1 (P@1): Measures whether the highest ranked precedent is an authoritative landmark case addressing the query.
  • Precision@3 & Recall@3: Evaluates top-3 candidate precision and comprehensive recall of related judicial opinions.
  • Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR): Reflects the ease of discovery by computing $1 / \text{rank}$ of the first relevant landmark authority.
